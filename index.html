FAQ

Questions people ask first

Do I need any experience to book?
No. A good part of the sessions are with people who have never worked in IT. Tell me where you're starting from in the booking form and I'll plan the hour around that.
What language will the session be in?
English, Portuguese or Spanish — whichever you prefer. Just pick it in the booking form.
Do I need to buy a Microsoft licence?
No. For anything hands-on we use a free trial tenant, which is enough for everything we'd cover in a session.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the session, using the link in your confirmation email. Cancellations more than 24 hours in advance are fully refunded.
Do you do migrations or consulting for my company?
No. This is individual mentoring and teaching only. I don't take on implementation, migration or support work for companies through this page.
What if 45 minutes isn't enough?
Then book another one when you need it. There's no package to commit to and no subscription — each session stands on its own.
